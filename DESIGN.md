# Design System: Tactical Dark Intelligence

## Palette
- Background: #060913 (Deep slate black)
- Accent: #00c7fc (Cyan), #ef4444 (Red), #f97316 (Orange), #10b981 (Emerald)
- Typography: Mono font, crisp uppercase tracking

## Component Principles
- High contrast, dark mode, tactical radar overlays
- Smooth transitions and zero visual pop/flicker during viewport navigation
